Our Story

A family kitchen inspired by mountain trails and valley markets — celebrating Sherpa, Tibetan and Nepali home cooking.

We combine traditional techniques with fresh, local ingredients sourced from surrounding valleys. Our chefs preserve authentic spice blends and slow-cooked flavors.

Open daily for lunch and dinner. Come for the momos, stay for the dal-bhat and the warm hospitality.
